KlipFarma

A private publishing tool for one YouTube channel.

KlipFarma is a personal tool used by the owner of a single YouTube channel to edit short clips from public Twitch streams (cutting, vertical framing, captions) and upload them to that owner's own channel.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and manage their privacy status, and the YouTube Analytics API to read the channel's own statistics. KlipFarma is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's YouTube account.

Streamers: want a clip removed?

Every video credits the streamer by name and links to their channel and the original clip. If you'd like a clip removed, email matejshorts@email.cz or leave a comment under the video. It will be taken down within 24 hours and no further clips of you will be posted.
