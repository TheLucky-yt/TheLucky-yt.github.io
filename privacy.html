Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

KlipFarma is a private tool used by the owner of one YouTube channel to upload edited clips to that channel. This policy explains what data KlipFarma accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

KlipFarma uses YouTube API Services. By using KlipFarma you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

  • Basic information about the connected YouTube channel (channel ID, title, subscriber and view counts).
  • The ability to upload videos to that channel and change their privacy status (for example, making a video private on request).
  • The channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, average view percentage, subscribers gained per video).

KlipFarma does not access any other user's account, private messages, contacts, or payment information.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish videos to the connected channel, to check their status, and to evaluate which videos perform well so future uploads can be improved. It is not used for advertising and is never sold or shared with third parties.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, encrypted with the operating system's per-user encryption (Windows DPAPI). Channel statistics are stored locally on the same computer. No data is stored on any external server operated by KlipFarma.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored statistics are kept only as long as needed to improve the channel and can be deleted at any time by deleting KlipFarma's data folder. You can revoke KlipFarma's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the stored token stops working.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.
